COLLECTING AND USING YOUR PERSONAL DATA
==================================================================

Types of Data Collected
-----------------------

Personal Data
~~~~~~~~~~~~~
While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain
personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or
identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is
not limited to:

- Email address, name and account identifier, used to create an Account
  and sign You in (via email/password or a social sign-in provider)
- Usage Data
- Health Information, as described below

Usage Data
~~~~~~~~~~
Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as the type of mobile device You
use, Your mobile device unique ID, the IP address of Your mobile
device, Your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet
browser You use, the app version, unique device identifiers, push
notification token and other diagnostic data.

Usage Data does not include Your Health Information.

Health Information and Backup
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The Application lets You record information about Your health,
including:

- Medications You add, such as names, strengths, doses, schedules
  (daily, specific weekdays, cyclical, as-needed, tapering) and linked
  pharmacy details
- Medication logs, such as when You take, skip or snooze a dose
- Vitals and health tracker entries, such as blood pressure, blood
  glucose, weight and heart rate, entered manually or synced from Apple
  Health / Google Fit with Your permission
- Side effects and symptoms, with severity and timestamps
- Inventory counts and refill dates

Stored with Our cloud provider. Your Health Information is stored by
Our cloud provider, Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest.
We use it only to operate the Service for You: to fire reminders, sync
Your data across Your devices, run interaction and allergy checks, and
deliver caregiver alerts You have allowed.

What We never do with Health Information. We never sell it. We never
use it for advertising. We never share it with advertising or analytics
partners, data brokers, or any third party except the Service Providers
listed in this policy that store it and run the Service for You.

Information from Apple Health and Google Fit
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
With Your permission, the Application can sync weight, heart rate and
other vitals from Apple Health or Google Fit. You can disconnect this
access at any time in Your Device settings or in the Application.

What We do not collect
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
We do not track Your location. We do not read Your contacts, messages or
photos. We do not collect advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA). We do not
collect any data for advertising profiles.

Use of Your Personal Data
-------------------------
The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain Our Service, including sending dose
  reminders, escalating unconfirmed reminders, and keeping the app
  working correctly
- To back up, synchronize and restore Your data across Your devices
- To show Your adherence calendar, streaks and history
- To warn You when stock runs low or a refill is due
- To check new medications against Your existing ones for interactions
  and against Your allergy list
- To generate the PDF/CSV reports You choose to export or share with
  Your doctor
- To send missed-dose alerts to a caregiver You have linked and given
  permission, including via SMS
- To keep Your account secure, including optional biometric app lock
- For the performance of a contract: the development, compliance and
  undertaking of the purchase contract for the products, items or
  services You have purchased or of any other contract with Us through
  the Service
- To contact You: by email, SMS, or push notifications regarding
  updates or informative communications related to the functionalities,
  products or contracted services, including security updates, when
  necessary or reasonable for their implementation
- To manage Your requests: to attend and manage Your requests to Us

We may share Your personal information in the following situations:

- With Service Providers: We may share Your personal information with
  Service Providers to store and process data on Our behalf, to send
  notifications, to process payments, and to contact You. We share
  Health Information only with the Service Providers that store it and
  run the Service for You, never with advertising or analytics partners.
- For Business transfers: We may share or transfer Your personal
  information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger,
  sale of Company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion
  of Our business to another company.

Caregiver and Family Sharing
----------------------------
Helping a family member or caregiver stay informed is a core part of
this Service, and it is built around consent:

- Linking requires consent. Sending an invite by email or SMS does not
  link anyone. The other person must accept before any data is shared.
- You choose the permission level. A Viewer can see adherence only. A
  Co-manager can also edit schedules and log doses on Your behalf.
- Sensitive data stays private by default. Vitals and side effects are
  visible only to people You have specifically given permission to see
  them.
- Missed-dose alerts are opt-in. A caregiver receives an alert about a
  missed dose only if alerts are turned on for that link.
- You can revoke access at any time. Removing a caregiver stops their
  access immediately.

One account can hold multiple profiles - Your own and family members
You manage - and each profile's data is shared only according to that
profile's permissions.

Retention of Your Personal Data
-------------------------------
The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is
necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will
retain and use Your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with
Our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce Our legal agreements
and policies.

While Your account is active, Your data is kept so that Your history,
adherence records and reports remain available to You.

When You delete Your account, Your personal and Health Information is
permanently deleted from Our live systems within 30 days. Encrypted
backup copies may persist for up to 90 days after deletion, after which
they are automatically purged. Backups are never used for anything
except disaster recovery.

Records We are legally required to keep - for example, subscription and
billing records held by the App Store, Google Play or RevenueCat - are
governed by those providers' own retention rules.

Transfer of Your Personal Data
------------------------------
Your information, including Personal Data, is processed at the Company's
operating offices and by Our Service Providers, including Google
Firebase, whose servers may be located outside of Your state, province,
country or other governmental jurisdiction where the data protection
laws may differ from those of Your jurisdiction.

Your consent to this Privacy Policy followed by Your submission of such
information represents Your agreement to that transfer.

The Company will take all steps reasonably necessary to ensure that Your
data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy and
no transfer of Your Personal Data will take place to an organization or
a country unless there are adequate controls in place including the
security of Your data and other personal information.

Security of Your Personal Data
------------------------------
- Encryption in transit and at rest: data moving between Your phone and
  Firebase is encrypted with TLS, and Health Information stored in
  Firebase is encrypted at rest.
- Per-user access rules: Firebase security rules ensure an account can
  only ever read its own data and data explicitly shared with it.
- Optional app lock: You can protect the app with Face ID or fingerprint
  unlock.
- No advertising SDKs: the Application contains no ad networks and no
  third-party tracking SDKs.

The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us, but remember that
no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic
storage is 100% secure. While We strive to use commercially acceptable
means to protect Your Personal Data, We cannot guarantee its absolute
security. If We ever discover a breach affecting Your data, We will
notify affected users and describe what happened and what We did about
